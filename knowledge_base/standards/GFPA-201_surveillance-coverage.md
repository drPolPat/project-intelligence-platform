# GFPA-201: Perimeter and Interior Surveillance Coverage Requirements

**Issuing body (fictional):** Global Facility Protection Alliance (GFPA)
**Edition:** 3rd Edition (Demonstration) — Fictional publication, not in force anywhere

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This standard establishes minimum closed-circuit video surveillance coverage requirements for facilities classified under the GFPA Facility Classification Scheme (see GFPA-001, not included in this demonstration corpus). It applies to perimeter boundaries, primary entry points, and interior common areas.

## 2. Facility Classifications Referenced

- **Class A — Public Assembly:** facilities with sustained public foot traffic, such as museums and retail centers.
- **Class B — Commercial Occupancy:** office and administrative buildings with controlled public access.
- **Class C — Critical Infrastructure:** unstaffed or minimally staffed utility and industrial sites.

## 3. Minimum Coverage Requirements

3.1. Class A facilities shall maintain video surveillance coverage of no less than **70%** of publicly accessible floor area, including all designated entry and exit points.

3.2. Class B facilities shall maintain coverage of no less than **55%** of common areas and 100% of ground-floor entry points.

3.3. Class C facilities shall maintain coverage of no less than **40%** of the perimeter fence line and 100% of vehicle and pedestrian access gates, recognizing that remote or unstaffed sites face greater deployment constraints.

3.4. Coverage percentage is calculated as the proportion of relevant area continuously recorded at a minimum resolution and frame rate sufficient for after-the-fact identification, as defined in GFPA-002 (not included in this demonstration corpus).

## 4. Recording Retention

4.1. Recorded footage shall be retained for a minimum of 30 days for Class A and Class B facilities, and 45 days for Class C facilities, unless a longer period is required by an applicable incident investigation.

## 5. Compliance Verification

5.1. Coverage percentage shall be re-verified at each scheduled safety inspection (see GFPA-240) and following any material change to facility layout, camera placement, or occupancy classification.

5.2. Facilities operating below the applicable minimum coverage threshold shall implement a documented remediation plan within 90 days of identification.

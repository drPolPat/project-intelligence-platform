# GFPA-240: Facility Safety Inspection Frequency, Scope, and Checklist Requirements

**Issuing body (fictional):** Global Facility Protection Alliance (GFPA)
**Edition:** 2nd Edition (Demonstration) — Fictional publication, not in force anywhere

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the minimum frequency, scope, and documentation requirements for scheduled facility safety inspections.

## 2. Minimum Inspection Frequency

2.1. Class A (Public Assembly) and Class B (Commercial Occupancy) facilities, as classified under GFPA-201 Section 2, shall undergo a full safety inspection no less than **twice per calendar year**.

2.2. Class C (Critical Infrastructure) facilities shall undergo a full safety inspection no less than **once per calendar year**, with an abbreviated interim inspection at the calendar-year midpoint covering fire/life-safety and surveillance items only.

2.3. Any facility that has undergone a Major or Critical incident (GFPA-230) shall receive a follow-up inspection within 30 days of the event, in addition to its regularly scheduled inspection.

## 3. Minimum Checklist Scope

3.1. Every scheduled inspection shall, at minimum, assess:

- Fire suppression and detection systems (extinguishers, alarms, sprinklers)
- Emergency egress (exit routes, signage, illumination)
- Structural condition, cross-referenced against open items under GFPA-215
- Perimeter and access-point security systems, cross-referenced against GFPA-201 and GFPA-250
- Evacuation planning and safety signage currency

3.2. Deficiencies identified during inspection shall be logged individually with a pass/fail determination and, where applicable, cross-referenced to the relevant structural vulnerability tier (GFPA-215) or surveillance/staffing gap (GFPA-201, GFPA-210).

## 4. Overall Result Determination

4.1. An inspection with zero deficiencies shall be recorded as **Pass**.

4.2. An inspection with 1–2 deficiencies shall be recorded as **Pass with Corrective Actions**, requiring documented follow-up and re-inspection of the affected items within 30 days.

4.3. An inspection with 3 or more deficiencies shall be recorded as **Fail**, requiring a full re-inspection within 30 days and notification of facility leadership.

## 5. Documentation

5.1. Inspection records, including inspector identity, date, full checklist results, and narrative notes, shall be retained for a minimum of 24 months.

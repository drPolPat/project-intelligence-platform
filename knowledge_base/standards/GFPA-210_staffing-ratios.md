# GFPA-210: Minimum Staffing Ratios for Physical Security Personnel

**Issuing body (fictional):** Global Facility Protection Alliance (GFPA)
**Edition:** 2nd Edition (Demonstration) — Fictional publication, not in force anywhere

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This standard establishes minimum on-site physical security staffing levels by facility classification (see GFPA-201, Section 2, for classification definitions), applicable during standard operating hours and adjusted for peak-occupancy periods.

## 2. Minimum Staffing Levels

2.1. Class A (Public Assembly) facilities shall maintain a minimum of **1 security staff member per 15,000 square feet** of publicly accessible area during operating hours, with a facility-wide minimum of 4 staff during peak occupancy periods.

2.2. Class B (Commercial Occupancy) facilities shall maintain a minimum of **1 security staff member per 25,000 square feet**, with a facility-wide minimum of 2 staff during standard business hours.

2.3. Class C (Critical Infrastructure) facilities that are not continuously staffed shall implement a documented remote-monitoring equivalency plan, including response-time commitments from a monitoring provider, in place of the staffing ratios in 2.1 and 2.2. Where on-site staffing is present, a minimum of 1 staff member shall be maintained per shift.

## 3. Staffing Gaps and Compensating Controls

3.1. A facility operating below its applicable minimum staffing ratio for more than 14 consecutive days shall document compensating controls, which may include enhanced surveillance coverage (GFPA-201), restricted after-hours access (GFPA-250), or temporary contracted security coverage.

3.2. Compensating controls do not exempt a facility from working toward restoration of the applicable minimum staffing ratio and shall be reviewed at each scheduled safety inspection (GFPA-240).

## 4. Training Requirements

4.1. All on-site physical security personnel shall complete facility-specific orientation covering evacuation procedures, incident reporting (GFPA-230), and access control protocols (GFPA-250) prior to independent duty.

4.2. Refresher training shall occur no less than annually.

## 5. Rationale

5.1. Staffing levels are understood by this standard to be a primary compensating factor against gaps in surveillance coverage and structural vulnerabilities; facilities with reduced staffing are expected to demonstrate strengthened controls in other areas covered by this framework.
